# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This app (SesnaQuake) respects your privacy.

## Data Collection and Usage

This App does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer's own servers.

This App uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) to count app launches. GoatCounter does
not use cookies, does not track you across sites or sessions, and does not store IP addresses or
any other personally identifiable information. See
[GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for details.

## Optional Third-Party Services

If you enable the optional Azure Speech, ElevenLabs, or OpenAI text-to-speech engines in Settings
using your own API key, the text of voice announcements is sent to that provider's servers to be
synthesized into audio. This is opt-in and disabled by default (the built-in OS/browser voice
engine is used instead). See [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement),
[ElevenLabs' privacy policy](https://elevenlabs.io/privacy), and
[OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/privacy/) for details.
